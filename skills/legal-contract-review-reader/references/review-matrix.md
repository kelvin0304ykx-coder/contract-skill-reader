# Contract Review Matrix

对付款依赖第三方回款、验收无期限、质保从验收起算、责任上限又被例外掏空等
组合，沿“触发条件→谁能控制→最迟何时结束→退出/救济→责任范围”联动审查。
分别标明效力命题、商业分配和待确认事实：效力敏感结论使用获准的渠道核对原文及
适用期间，不凭模型常识先排除核验。给出彼此兼容的替代文字，核对附件和例外
是否撤销保护；甲乙方立场改变应影响建议，不能改变事实或已经核验的法源。

Use this matrix for a full redline or finalization review. Apply only sections relevant to the transaction. Treat it as a scenario-testing guide, not a clause library.

## 1. Document Integrity And Hierarchy

- Identify the exact version, drafting party, existing revisions/comments, missing pages, duplicated clauses, blanks, and template residue.
- Confirm the order of precedence among the main contract, orders, statements of work, technical specifications, quotations, procurement files, response documents, award notices, meeting minutes, and later written changes.
- Check defined terms, party abbreviations, clause numbering, cross-references, tables, formulas, annex names, page references, and signature blocks.
- Flag an annex that carries essential scope, price, acceptance, or technical content but is absent, unsigned, or inconsistent.
- Do not assume a generic incorporation clause solves an actual contradiction between documents.

## 2. Parties And Authority

- Confirm each party's contractual role, legal capacity, authority chain, branch/head-office relationship, agent authority, and guarantor or affiliate responsibility.
- Check whether performance requires a license, permit, qualification, filing, registration, professional personnel, or third-party authorization.
- Check whether payment recipient, invoice issuer, performance entity, IP owner, data processor, and signing entity are the same or properly connected.
- Treat identity, registration, account, authority, and qualification gaps as fact checks unless verified from current sources.
- Do not repair unsupported party information directly in the contract body.

## 3. Subject, Scope, And Performance

- Define deliverables, quantity, quality, functions, technical standards, service levels, locations, milestones, personnel, working hours, and exclusions.
- Check whether obligations are measurable and achievable rather than left to one party's subjective satisfaction.
- Identify dependencies on sites, systems, data, approvals, access, materials, personnel, or third parties and allocate delay consequences.
- Control additional work, oral instructions, scope creep, rework, and change orders.
- Check subcontracting, assignment, replacement of key personnel, and responsibility for affiliates or vendors.
- Preserve reasonable cure opportunities without allowing indefinite delay.

## 4. Price, Tax, Invoice, And Payment

- Reconcile total price, unit price, tax-inclusive/exclusive wording, tax rate assumptions, currency, formula, rounding, and annex tables.
- Distinguish fixed price, estimated quantity, framework ceiling, reimbursable cost, milestone price, and price-adjustment mechanisms.
- Ensure payment milestones correspond to provable performance and acceptance events.
- Test whether payment is conditioned on an event controlled solely by the paying party or an unrelated third party.
- Check invoice type, timing, correction, tax-rate change, invoice non-compliance, and whether invoice disputes improperly defeat the underlying payment obligation.
- Check retention, deposit, advance payment, security, performance bond, set-off, deduction, audit adjustment, reconciliation, and refund.
- Avoid clauses that permit unlimited withholding or deduction without notice, evidence, dispute procedure, or cap.

## 5. Delivery, Acceptance, And Risk Transfer

- Specify delivery method, location, recipient, documentation, system record, custody, title, and risk-transfer point.
- Define acceptance standards, required materials, process, participants, time limit, written result, defect classification, retest, and re-performance.
- Test whether silence means acceptance, rejection, or no result; align deemed acceptance with the user's role and leverage.
- Separate technical acceptance, operational use, final acceptance, warranty start, invoice eligibility, and payment eligibility when they serve different functions.
- Prevent use, partial payment, or a preliminary confirmation from producing unintended final acceptance unless commercially intended.
- Give the supplier a remedy when acceptance is delayed by missing cooperation, while preserving the buyer's rights for latent defects.

## 6. Change, Cooperation, And Dependencies

- Require identifiable authorized personnel and a recordable change process for scope, price, schedule, standards, and personnel.
- State whether email, platform instruction, meeting minutes, work order, or signed amendment can change the contract.
- Allocate consequences of late instructions, unavailable data, site conditions, approval delays, third-party interfaces, and regulatory changes.
- Avoid requiring performance of disputed additional work without a pricing or reservation mechanism.
- Check order cancellation, volume fluctuation, minimum purchase, exclusivity, forecast, and no-commitment clauses.

## 7. Warranty, Breach, And Remedies

- Define warranty scope, start, duration, response, repair/replacement, service levels, exclusions, and repeated-defect consequences.
- Match liquidated damages to the relevant obligation, trigger, calculation base, accrual period, cap, and proof position.
- Check whether damages, liquidated damages, indemnity, replacement, refund, specific performance, termination, and set-off are cumulative, alternative, exclusive, or internally contradictory.
- Review liability exclusions and caps for asymmetry, carve-outs, indirect-loss wording, data/IP/confidentiality breaches, personal injury, fraud, gross negligence, and third-party claims.
- Require notice, defense control, settlement consent, mitigation, evidence, and allocation for indemnity claims.
- Avoid unlimited liability created indirectly by broad promises, warranties, compliance clauses, or uncapped daily penalties.
- Check both under-remedy and over-remedy: a clause can be commercially unusable even when legally valid.

## 8. Suspension, Termination, And Settlement

- Distinguish termination for cause, convenience, prolonged force majeure, insolvency, regulatory prohibition, repeated breach, and change of control.
- Define cure notice, cure period, immediate-termination events, suspension rights, and continued obligations.
- State settlement on exit: completed work, work in progress, accepted/unaccepted deliverables, prepaid amounts, refunds, transition assistance, data/material return, personnel withdrawal, and costs.
- Check whether termination rights are unilateral or illusory because the trigger cannot be proved.
- Preserve accrued payment, confidentiality, IP, data, audit, indemnity, evidence, and dispute clauses as appropriate.
- Avoid termination language that conflicts with automatic renewal, minimum term, order commitments, or exclusivity.

## 9. Confidentiality, Data, And Intellectual Property

- Define confidential information, exclusions, permitted users, purpose, security, compelled disclosure, duration, return/destruction, and proof of breach.
- Allocate background IP, project deliverables, tools, improvements, third-party materials, open-source components, licenses, source code, and moral-right issues.
- Match ownership or license timing to payment and acceptance when appropriate.
- For data, identify roles, purpose, scope, source, consent/authorization, processing instructions, security, breach response, retention, deletion, cross-border transfer, and subcontractors.
- Check whether audit, monitoring, publicity, case-study, logo, training-data, or model-use rights exceed the transaction need.
- Avoid promising rights the contracting party does not own or cannot sublicense.

## 10. Compliance And Special Regimes

- Identify mandatory rules and qualifications for procurement, state assets, employment, construction, medical, finance, safety, food/drug, environment, data, cybersecurity, export control, sanctions, anti-bribery, or industry regulation.
- Check whether a generic compliance promise creates unlimited strict liability for all laws, policies, future changes, affiliates, and subcontractors.
- In procurement-related contracts, compare the contract with procurement requirements, response commitments, award results, approved budget, scoring promises, and prohibited substantive changes.
- Check inspection, audit, reporting, record retention, regulator access, and cooperation obligations for scope, cost, notice, confidentiality, and privilege.
- Form the preliminary clause and transaction judgment first. When material, verify current law, exceptions and adverse authority through an available authorized original-text channel, including applicable version, validity and precise locator. Follow [research-sources.md](research-sources.md). Once sufficient, do not repeat searches; otherwise mark only the affected point `需检索确认`.
- Do not run counterparty company lookup in ordinary contract review. Check subject and qualification facts only against supplied materials and use comments for missing or inconsistent facts; reserve company lookup for an express separate due-diligence request.

## 11. Evidence, Notice, And Dispute Resolution

- Identify what record proves each major event: instruction, delivery, acceptance, objection, defect, cure, invoice, payment, breach, notice, termination, and loss.
- Define authorized contacts, addresses, email/platform channels, change notices, deemed receipt, failed delivery, and litigation/arbitration service where appropriate.
- Avoid a notice mechanism that cannot be preserved or conflicts with actual operations.
- Check governing law, negotiation prerequisites, escalation, mediation, arbitration institution/seat/rules, court jurisdiction, preservation, injunctive relief, costs, and language.
- Confirm the dispute clause is complete, valid, internally consistent, and appropriate for enforcement and evidence location.
- Do not assume a chosen court has jurisdiction without a connecting factor and current-law check where required.

## 12. Annexes, Signature, And Final Consistency

- Reconcile contract name, party names, dates, amounts, terms, annex list, technical tables, payment schedule, contacts, and signature pages.
- Confirm every referenced annex exists and uses the same identifiers as the body.
- Check execution method, copies, seal/signature requirements, effective date, conditions precedent, electronic signature, and later order formation.
- Remove unresolved placeholders and internal drafting notes only when they are not intentional user comments.
- Preserve the user's deliberate deletions and settled wording unless a material issue requires reopening it.

## 13. Transaction Scenario Tests

Run the scenarios that fit the contract:

- `ordinary performance`: Can both sides tell exactly what to do next and what record proves completion?
- `delay`: Who caused it, what notice is required, how is time extended, and what remedy applies?
- `defect`: Who decides, under what standard, within what period, with what cure and payment consequence?
- `non-payment`: What condition is unmet, who controls it, what evidence exists, and can performance be suspended or terminated?
- `scope change`: Who can order it, when does price/time change, and what happens if work starts before agreement?
- `third-party claim`: Who controls defense, settlement, evidence, mitigation, and allocation?
- `regulatory change`: Is performance adjusted, suspended, repriced, or terminated, and who bears sunk cost?
- `early exit`: What is paid, returned, transferred, deleted, preserved, and still enforceable?
- `dispute`: Can the user prove the claim, serve notice, preserve evidence, obtain interim relief, and enforce the result?

## 14. Observable Quality Gates

Record these internal pass/fail gates before delivery:

- `preliminary_judgment_completed`: the transaction map, broken loops, adverse scenarios, and proposed actions were formed before external research.
- `adverse_authority_checked_if_material`: current exceptions and adverse authority were checked when they could materially change an external legal conclusion; any unavailable item is identified precisely.
- `counterparty_company_lookup_not_run`: no company-information route was used unless the user expressly requested separate company due diligence.
- `cross_deliverable_mapping_complete`: each current material issue uses the same change ID wherever it appears in requested, applicable deliverables, without creating redundant output; no user-deleted, abandoned, or superseded change remains current elsewhere.
